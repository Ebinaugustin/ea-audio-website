A NOTE FROM THE CREATOR

A Message
to the Developers.

Free.
Forever.

First, I want to make something clear: I am not a professional plugin developer or a coder. I’m an audio professional who enjoys experimenting with ideas, exploring new possibilities, and sharing the tools I create along the way.

Every plugin I share here is free, and will remain free forever. I’m not doing this to compete with commercial developers or to undermine the incredible work they do. Quite the opposite — I have immense respect for the people who dedicate their time, knowledge, creativity, and countless hours of hard work to building audio plugins.

AI has made it easier for people like me to experiment with ideas that might otherwise have remained just ideas. But generating code and building a truly innovative, reliable, and well-engineered plugin are two very different things. Behind great software is a level of understanding, problem-solving, experience, and creativity that deserves recognition.

To the human developers out there: we need you. Your innovations, your expertise, and your passion are what push this industry forward. AI can be a powerful tool, but it is not a replacement for the ingenuity of people who know their craft and dare to build something new.

These plugins are simply my way of experimenting, learning, and sharing something useful with the audio community.

Much respect to every developer who makes the tools we use possible. Keep innovating. The audio world needs you.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
step inside.

What is RoomMatch?+

RoomMatch is EA Audio’s first audio plugin: an experimental image-to-reverb tool. Load a room photo, generate an impulse response, and shape its playback with reverb and spatial controls.

Does it recreate the photographed room exactly?+

No. The photo model suggests an acoustic starting point. Room dimensions, materials, and positions are manually adjustable, and the image is not a metric reconstruction or acoustic measurement.

Which formats and platforms are available?+

RoomMatch beta is available for Windows x64, Mac Apple Silicon, and Mac Intel. Each package includes a VST3 plugin and a standalone application.

Does photo analysis need an internet connection?+

The complete packages include the AI model and runtime for offline inference. First-time preparation can take a few minutes. See the release notes for installation details.

How do I install RoomMatch?+

Close your DAW before installing. On Windows, extract the complete ZIP and run Install RoomMatch.cmd. On Mac, choose the PKG for Apple Silicon or Intel. The Mac packages are not Apple notarized. See the release notes for installation details.
